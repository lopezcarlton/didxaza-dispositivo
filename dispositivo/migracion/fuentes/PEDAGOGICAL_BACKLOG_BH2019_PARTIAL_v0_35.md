# PEDAGOGICAL_BACKLOG_BH2019_PARTIAL_v0_35

Status: `PROVISIONAL_BACKLOG_NOT_AUTOMATICALLY_EXECUTABLE`

This backlog records pedagogical consequences that became visible during the partial BH2019 reading. It does not rewrite COR002 automatically and does not treat the source as a complete contemporary grammar.

## A. Safe structural changes already adopted

- conversations may preserve discourse state without making it mandatory for isolated analysis;
- acquisition records may preserve conversation/turn/IU hierarchy;
- elicitation method must be distinguishable;
- raw evidence remains separate from later discourse annotations;
- isolated utterances remain first-class pedagogical and Analyzer objects.

## B. Pedagogical design requiring later adjudication

- design information arcs, not only thematic arcs;
- distinguish new/given/reintroduced referents;
- practice introduction, maintenance, switch and return of referents;
- include more than one third-person referent in selected scenes;
- distinguish predicate/sentence/argument focus;
- create minimal pragmatic contrasts with same event but different question/context;
- teach preverbal position as construction-sensitive rather than `preverbal = focus`;
- distinguish topic from focus;
- treat `nga` only after construction-specific adjudication;
- preserve natural prosodic units in audio when available;
- expose learners eventually to isolated forms, intonation units and complete turns;
- use spontaneous/naturalistic and nonlinguistic elicitation alongside deliberately labeled translation elicitation;
- do not equate rarity with invalidity or elicited possibility with conversational naturalness;
- review pragmatic difficulty independently from grammatical difficulty and turn count.

## C. Explicitly deferred

- no automatic P1–P5 rewrite yet;
- no new productive focus/topic generator rules yet;
- no `=be`/zero automatic correction rule;
- no `nga` global semantic mapping;
- no mandatory discourse-state schema for every corpus row;
- no mandatory prosodic annotation;
- no long-distance discourse module opened during NC001 solely because BH2019 describes the phenomenon.
